# Dhadkan: Requirements

## Problem
Bystanders often freeze or act on wrong information during cardiac
emergencies, and connectivity is unreliable in many places in India.

## Users
- A bystander with a phone, possibly offline, possibly panicking.
- A person aged roughly 23-56 who wants to understand their own
  cardio-metabolic risk.

## Use cases
1. Someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally: get CPR steps fast.
2. Someone has chest pain: get recognition and first-response steps.
3. A user checks their risk factors and gets a "consider screening" summary.

## Non-goals
- No diagnosis.
- No drug dosing; no prescription drugs.
- No emergencies beyond cardiac.
- No storing personal data.

## Safety requirements
- Every step traces to a published source.
- "Call 112" is always the first instruction and cannot be overridden by content.
- Content carries a review record; a clinician reviews before release.

## Success criteria
- Works offline on a phone in airplane mode.
- A clinician has reviewed and signed off the protocols.
