  Kapoor MC, Singh B
- Title: Compression-only life support (COLS) for cardiopulmonary
  resuscitation by layperson outside the hospital
- Publisher: Indian Journal of Anaesthesia, 2017 Nov; 61(11): 867-873
  (guideline of the Resuscitation Council formed by the Indian Society of
  Anaesthesiologists)
- DOI: 10.4103/ija.IJA_636_17
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5702999/  (confirm it opens)
- Accessed: 2026-10-29
- Used for: cardiac arrest protocol (algorithm, rate, depth, hand position)
- TO VERIFY: read the full paper; check my draft steps against it, not just
  against the CBSE workbook.

## S3: Emergency Response Support System (ERSS), emergency number 112

- URL: https://www.mha.gov.in/emergency-response-support-system-erss
- Accessed: 2026-10-29
- Used for: 112 as India's single emergency number
